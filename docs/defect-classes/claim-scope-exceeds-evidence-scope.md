# Claim scope exceeds evidence scope (population-scope generalization)

**Status: FIRST LOGGED OCCURRENCE — not yet promoted.** Per the accretion doctrine a
close-discipline bullet enters the served block only on repeated LOGGED failure; a second
occurrence of this class promotes a population-scope bullet (the sibling of the staged
host-scope bullet — same family: evidence silently generalized past what produced it).

## The class

A claim's **scope** silently exceeds its **evidence's** scope: proof obtained from one member of
a population is recorded as a property of the population. The claim is not fabricated — the
evidence is real, the inference is the defect. It survives review because every reader checks
"is the evidence real?" (yes) and nobody checks "does the evidence's scope match the claim's?"

**The rule: a claim's scope may not exceed its evidence's scope.** One seat's proof supports
"this seat hydrates," never "the fleet hydrates." n-of-n claims need n-of-n evidence.

## Detection move

For every readiness/deployment claim, ask: *over what population is this claim quantified, and
over what population was the evidence collected?* If the two differ, the claim is [U] regardless
of how good the evidence is for its actual scope. Grep-shaped hunt: readiness statements
containing "fleet-wide", "all", "every", "13/13" — trace each to the evidence artefact and count
its targets.
